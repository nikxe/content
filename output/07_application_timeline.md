# Application Timeline — Week-by-Week (2026-05-04 → 2026-08-31)

**Today:** 2026-05-04 (Monday).
**Latest deadline in shortlist:** 2026-08-31 (TU Berlin MSc CS).
**Earliest deadline:** 2026-05-15 (OVGU Visual Computing, OVGU Digital Engineering, TU Ilmenau RCSE) — **11 days from today.**

This timeline assumes you act on the recommended top-10 apply list from `04_shortlist_final.md`. If you also apply to programs 11–28, the documents and uni-assist account work scale up, but the dates do not change.

Buffer assumptions:
- **uni-assist VPD turnaround:** 4–6 weeks from submission of all required docs.
- **DHL Shimla→Germany express:** 5–7 business days; allow 10 days for safety.
- **LOR turnaround:** 2–3 weeks from request (approach professors / managers immediately).
- **SOP drafting cycle:** 2 weeks (1 week first draft + 1 week revision).
- **IELTS TRF copies:** ~5 working days from the TRF Online portal once Test Report Form is issued.
- **Visa appointment lead time at German consulates (Delhi / Mumbai / Bangalore):** 2–3 months.
