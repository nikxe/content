## Executive summary

Out of 185 English-taught CS-discipline master's programs surveyed at 53 German public universities, **28 programs across 21 universities pass all 12 hard filters and remain open for WS 26/27 application**. Major exclusions you will not find in this shortlist: TUM (€6,000/sem tuition), all FAU programs (newly require GRE/GATE for non-EU starting WS 26/27, plus a 3-application cap), Saarland (GRE/GATE for non-EHEA), KIT MSc CS (GRE/GATE), Konstanz (GRE/GMAT), Paderborn (GRE/dMat with no waiver at 2.4 GPA), Bayreuth (GRE/GATE for non-EHEA — newly discovered, contradicts Section 12 brief which only flagged the GPA cutoff), all TU Chemnitz programs (German A1 required at admission, not just enrollment), Augsburg CS / CSiE (German A2 at admission), Tübingen MSc CS (German B2-C1 for international), and most of the North-German universities (Lübeck, Hamburg IAS, Bremen AIIS, TU Braunschweig CSE — all had non-EU deadlines in February to April that have already passed). RWTH, RPTU Kaiserslautern and Stuttgart are also out for WS 26/27 — applicant should consider their Summer 2027 windows as a Plan B.

**If you only have time for 5 applications,** apply to: **(1) TU Darmstadt MSc Computer Science** (best overall fit, AI/Visual Computing/SE all strong, English-only, no GRE, MOI accepted, 15 Jul deadline, paper docs by post — start now), **(2) HPI Potsdam MSc Computer Science** (Reach with strong AI track, free, direct portal, 1 Jun deadline — only 27 days), **(3) Universität Bonn MSc Computer Science** (top brand for AI/ML/Visual Computing via Lamarr Institute, but selective; 1 Jun single-round non-EEA window — apply ASAP), **(4) TU Berlin MSc Computer Science** (broad CS, latest deadline 31 Aug giving longest runway; VPD via uni-assist required), and **(5) OVGU Magdeburg MSc Visual Computing** (best Visual Computing fit with explicit GPA 2.5 cutoff applicant clears; 15 May deadline — extremely tight, must apply within 11 days).

**If you have time for 10–15 applications,** add safer Target picks: TU Dresden MSc CS, Universität Marburg MSc CS, Universität Passau MSc CS, Universität Regensburg MSc CS, Universität Bielefeld IISY (strong AI), Universität Potsdam MSc CS, Leibniz Universität Hannover MSc CS, BTU Cottbus MSc AI (Safety with AI focus), and TU Darmstadt MSc AIML (Reach for AI focus, applies through same TU Darmstadt portal as MSc CS).

## What could break the plan

- **VPD turnaround delay.** uni-assist VPD takes 4–6 weeks. If you submit your VPD request after 2026-05-15, you risk missing the 2026-06-01 deadlines (Bonn, HPI, Uni Potsdam, Regensburg). **Action:** initiate VPD this week.
- **Anti-bot blocking on official pages.** During verification, many official German university pages returned 403 to automated fetchers. The deadlines and rules in this shortlist were verified via WebSearch on official `*.de` domains where direct fetch failed. Each program's primary URL is in the shortlist for you to confirm in a browser before submitting.
- **Deadlines change without notice.** Several programs (Tübingen ML, RWTH non-EU, Stuttgart MSc CS, Frankfurt MSc Inf) were closed before their nominal deadlines because of a separate non-EU window we missed in initial inventory. Always re-verify the deadline on the program page in the days before you submit.
- **Bayreuth-style hidden GRE rules.** Bayreuth's GRE/GATE-for-non-EHEA rule was not in the original brief's known-pitfalls list — agents found it in Phase 3. There may be similar hidden rules at programs we did not fully verify. Treat any program with GPA cutoff or unusual structure as worth a clarifying email.
- **TU Darmstadt paper-docs deadline.** TU Darmstadt requires paper documents to be **received** by 15 July, not postmarked. DHL Shimla→Germany is 5–7 business days. Mail by 1 July to be safe.
- **MOI letter rejected.** Bonn explicitly does NOT accept MOI; LUH and HPI do. Have IELTS 7.0 certificate ready as the universal proof. (Applicant has IELTS 7.0 — this is fine, but TOEFL backup is wise if any program demands it.)
- **Visa appointment lead time.** German consulate (Delhi/Mumbai/Bangalore) student visa appointments are 2–3 months. **Action:** book a visa appointment slot the moment a first admit arrives — do not wait for all results.
