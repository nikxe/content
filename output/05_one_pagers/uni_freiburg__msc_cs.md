## Why this is on the shortlist

Freiburg is a U15 university with one of Europe's strongest ML groups (Frank Hutter, AutoML), a recognised Computer Vision Lab, and a robotics group. The MSc Computer Science has a named **AI specialisation** and a named **Cyber-Physical Systems** track. The Open Curriculum option allows the applicant to mix AI/ML, Visual Computing, and Robotics electives without locking a track. Trade-off: Baden-Württemberg charges €1,500/sem for non-EU — at the edge of applicant tolerance.

## Specializations / tracks

- **AI specialisation** — Frank Hutter's AutoML chair, ML systems chair.
