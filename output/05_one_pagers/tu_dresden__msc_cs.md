## Why this is on the shortlist

TU Dresden is a TU9 institution with strong AI, Visual Computing, Software Engineering, and Theoretical CS chairs. The MSc Computer Science offers a Visual Computing track, an AI/ML track, and a Distributed Systems Engineering track (which absorbed the standalone MSc DSE). Saxony state has free tuition. Compared to Bonn, TU Dresden is moderately less selective and more accessible to a 2.4 GPA applicant.

## Specializations / tracks

- **GRE / GATE:** **Strongly recommended for non-EU/EEA but not strictly required.** Soft penalty without it.
- **APS:** Required.

## Risks and caveats

- **GRE strongly recommended.** Without GRE, applicant's chances are reduced. Compensate with strong SOP and clear research interest in TU Dresden chairs.
- **27-day deadline + 4–6 week VPD.** Submit application before VPD if needed.
- **Foreign-HZB routing.** Don't be misled by direct-application appearance — must go through uni-assist first (Section 12 pitfall).
- **Aptitude assessment.** TU Dresden runs an internal aptitude review of Bachelor content; applicants whose Bachelor's CS content is "thin" may be rejected even with 2.4 GPA.
