## Why this is on the shortlist

TU Ilmenau bypasses uni-assist — direct application + €75 fee paid to the university. The MSc RCSE is research-oriented with a strong SSE (Software & Systems Engineering) group and explicit CS+EE+automation crossover. The applicant clears the (modest) IELTS B2 requirement easily with IELTS 7.0. Fast lane for the urgent 15 May cluster: no VPD wait.
