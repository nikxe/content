- **Application deadline (non-EU):** 2026-06-01 — **27 days from today**
- **Application route:** Direct (HPI portal, NOT uni-assist)
- **Composite score:** 48 / 55 — **Reach tier**

## Why this is on the shortlist

HPI is the strongest direct-route, English-only, free-tuition program in the shortlist. Hosts the Hasso Plattner Institute itself plus collaborations with SAP. The MSc CS programme has four named tracks aligned with the applicant's interest order: **Data & AI** (best for AI/ML), **Cybersecurity** (Security Engineering track), **Systems Engineering** (SE-aligned), and **Digital Health**. Founded by Hasso Plattner (SAP co-founder) and known for tightly-supervised, project-heavy curriculum.

## Specializations / tracks

- **Cybersecurity** — Security Engineering chair (Meinel et al.).
- **Data & AI** — analytics, ML, big data systems (Plattner ML chairs).
- **Systems Engineering** — distributed systems, cloud, scalable systems.
- **Digital Health** — health informatics applications.

## Admission requirements

- **English level:** IELTS 7.0 overall AND ≥6.5 in each section. TOEFL iBT 95 acceptable. MOI letter accepted ("degree from accredited university with English as sole language of instruction" exempts).
- **GPA cutoff:** No published cutoff but selective.
- **ECTS prerequisites:** 180 CP Bachelor in CS / IT-Systems Engineering / Data Science. ≥15 CP math (Discrete Structures, Logic, Analysis, Linear Algebra, Stochastics). ≥10 CP Technical CS (OS, Computer Architecture, Distributed Systems).
- **GRE / GATE:** Not required.
- **APS:** Required.

## Application logistics

- **Portal:** Direct on HPI's own application system at https://hpi.de/en/studies/application/application-computer-science-msc/.
- **Deadline:** 1 June 2026.
- **Fee:** €0.

- **Paper docs:** Likely uploaded scans only.
- **VPD:** Not required (HPI processes foreign documents internally).

## Risks and caveats

- **Selective admission.** HPI runs small cohorts (~50/year per track in some recent years). No published admit rate — apply with strong SOP.
- **27-day deadline.** Tighter than the 15 May cluster but still demanding. Begin SOP and HPI account this week.
- **Tracks chosen at application or later?** HPI states the four track names; verify whether the applicant must commit at application or in semester 2 (Q10 in `11_open_questions.md`).
- **Same-day deadline cluster as Bonn / Uni Potsdam / Regensburg.** Plan to submit HPI by 2026-05-25 to avoid same-week portal issues.
