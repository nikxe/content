## Why this is on the shortlist

Bonn hosts the Lamarr Institute for ML and AI, a German Excellence-strategy AI hub jointly with TU Dortmund and Fraunhofer IAIS. Its MSc Computer Science is one of the strongest programs in NRW for AI / ML / Visual Computing. Among the four named tracks, **Graphics/Vision/Audio** is the highest-scoring Visual Computing alignment in the shortlist outside of TU Darmstadt. Bonn also hosts the Bonn-Aachen International Center for IT (b-it) collaboration with RWTH.

## Specializations / tracks

- **Algorithmics** — theory, algorithms, complexity.
- **Graphics, Vision, and Audio** — Visual Computing fit.
- **Information & Communication Management** — networks, distributed systems.
- **Intelligent Systems** — AI/ML, knowledge representation, ML systems.

## Admission requirements

- **English level:** IELTS 7.0 OR TOEFL (specific minimum unverified). **No MOI substitute** — Bachelor-in-English does not exempt.
- **GPA cutoff:** No published cutoff but admit rate <5% for international applicants per WebSearch.
- **ECTS prerequisites:**
  - 18 ECTS math foundations
  - 14 ECTS algorithms / complexity
  - 18 ECTS programming / software engineering
  - Bachelor thesis ≥12 ECTS on a CS topic
- **GRE / GATE:** Not required, but selectivity is high — strong SOP and clear ECTS audit needed.
- **APS:** Required.

## Risks and caveats

- **<5% admit rate.** With GPA 2.4 and no GRE, applicant is competing against a global pool of ML/CS applicants. SOP and ECTS audit must be flawless.
- **No MOI substitute.** IELTS 7.0 must be uploaded. TOEFL backup recommended (not strictly needed if IELTS 7.0 valid).
- **Strict ECTS audit.** Bonn explicitly checks for 18+14+18+12 ECTS in specific subject groups — applicant must produce a precise course-by-course mapping. UTU registrar may need to issue an ECTS-converted transcript.
- **Single non-EEA round.** No second chance after 1 June 2026 for WS 26/27.
- **Tight deadline.** uni-assist VPD may not be ready by 1 June if VPD not initiated by 2026-05-04 — submit application without VPD if needed and let uni-assist + Bonn process the VPD in parallel.
