## Why this is on the shortlist

The applicant's stated #1 interest is AI/ML. TU Darmstadt's MSc AIML is one of two named "AI / ML" master's programs at a German public university accessible to this applicant (Bayreuth's MSc CS with AI track was excluded due to GRE/GATE rule; Saarland's DSAI excluded by GRE/GATE; FAU's MSc AI excluded by GRE/GATE; Tübingen's MSc ML closed and 2.0 cutoff). TU Darmstadt MSc AIML benefits from the entire CS faculty's AI strength (hessian.AI, DFKI, Centre Responsible Digitality) and shares admission with MSc CS — submit one courier package, two applications.

## Specializations / tracks

The MSc AIML is the named specialisation. Within it, electives cover:
- **Machine Learning** — supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement learning.
- **Deep Learning** — neural architectures, generative models.
- **AI Theory** — formal foundations.
- **Robotics & Autonomous Systems** — joint with mech eng.
- **Natural Language Processing** — applied ML for text.
- **Computer Vision** — bridges with the Visual Computing electives.

## Admission requirements

Same as MSc CS:
- **English level:** IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL iBT 95. MOI accepted.
- **GPA cutoff:** None published. Entrance Exam if competency unclear.

- **GRE / GATE:** Not required, but valid scores may exempt from Entrance Exam.
- **APS:** Required.
