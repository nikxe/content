## Why this is on the shortlist

LUH is a TU9-adjacent (was TU9 candidate) U15 research university with the L3S Research Center for AI/ML/data systems. Strong AI signal but moderate Visual Computing and Software Engineering depth. The MSc CS is large (broad Bachelor admission base) and the requirement of "C1 in English OR C1 in German" with English-only graduation path is realistic for the applicant.

## Specializations / tracks

- **Data Science** — analytics, ML systems.
- **Human-Centered Computing** — HCI, accessibility, user systems.
- **Systems-oriented CS** — distributed, embedded, networks.

## Risks and caveats

- **VPD lead-time risk.** 27 days to deadline; VPD takes 4–6 weeks. Submit uni-assist application even before VPD is fully ready — uni-assist will forward VPD when issued.
- **Some German modules.** Verify that the elective catalog you want is fully English-deliverable. The 2nd application via Q in `11_open_questions.md` is not strictly necessary since LUH explicitly states English graduation is feasible.
- **Aptitude test.** Internal review of Bachelor content; emphasise CS-heavy modules from BTech IT.
- **AI Mech Robotics second application.** Add LUH MSc AI-driven Mechatronics & Robotics to the same uni-assist programme list (extra €30) as a complementary application.
