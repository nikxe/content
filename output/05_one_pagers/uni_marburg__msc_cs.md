## Why this is on the shortlist

Marburg is admission-free (no NC) which means the bottleneck is meeting prerequisites, not competing on GPA. The MSc CS has a named **AI specialisation** and electives across cybersecurity, software engineering, data systems, and theoretical CS. Applicant's GPA 2.4 should clear easily; the internal eligibility assessment is more about the 90 ECTS in math+CS rule (12 math + 72 CS minimum), which BTech IT typically meets.

- **GRE / GATE:** Not required.
