## Why this is on the shortlist

TU Darmstadt is the highest-composite program for this applicant: AI/ML (5/5), Visual Computing (5/5), Software Engineering (5/5), and General CS breadth (5/5) all maxed. Hosts hessian.AI and shares affiliation with DFKI and the Centre Responsible Digitality — among the strongest AI/ML research clusters in Europe. The MSc CS curriculum lets the applicant choose Data Science & Engineering, Visual Computing, OR Distributed Computing as a specialisation, covering all top three of the applicant's stated interest order. Direct application route bypasses uni-assist entirely.

## Specializations / tracks

- **Data Science & Engineering** — ML, data engineering, statistical methods.
- **Visual Computing** — computer graphics, vision, geometry processing (replaces the standalone MSc Visual Computing closed in 2023).
- **Distributed Computing** — distributed systems, cloud, parallelism (replaces the standalone MSc DSS closed in 2023).
- Generic CS path also available without locking a specialisation at admission.

## Admission requirements

- **English level:** IELTS Academic 7.0 OR TOEFL iBT 95 (CBT 213 / paper 550). MOI letter accepted as alternative. _Source: program page._
- **GPA cutoff:** No published numerical cutoff. Admission is based on competency match (ECTS in CS subjects) and an Entrance Exam in early September if competencies are not directly verifiable from transcripts.
- **ECTS prerequisites:** 180 CP Bachelor with ≥60 CP comparable to the TU Darmstadt B.Sc. CS curriculum (algorithms, programming, OS, databases, networks, software engineering).
- **GRE / GATE policy:** Not required. **Valid GRE or GATE scores may exempt the applicant from the Entrance Exam** — confirm thresholds via email (see `11_open_questions.md`, Q2).
- **APS:** Required for Indian applicants.

## Risks and caveats

- **Paper docs by post.** No postmark grace; package must physically arrive by 15 July. DHL Shimla → Darmstadt is 5–7 business days; allow buffer.
- **Entrance Exam in September.** If TU Darmstadt cannot verify competency from transcripts alone, the applicant may be required to take an Entrance Exam in early September 2026 — schedule conflicts with visa appointments.
- **2 applications, 1 portal.** MSc CS and MSc AIML use the same TUCaN portal — same package, same courier shipment.
- **Hessen state, no tuition** — but a semester contribution of ~€270 covers public transit (Semesterticket), useful in this region.
