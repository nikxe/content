## Why this is on the shortlist

OVGU Magdeburg's MSc Visual Computing is the strongest **named Visual Computing degree** accessible to this applicant — Saarland VC is excluded by GRE/GATE; Siegen ETI tracks have closed deadlines; TU Darmstadt has Visual Computing as a track within MSc CS rather than a separate degree. OVGU explicitly publishes a 2.5 GPA cutoff which the applicant clears with 2.4. The IELTS 7.0 requirement is met. The named program covers visualization, computer graphics, and computer vision.
