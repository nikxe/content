# uni-assist Strategy

**Date built:** 2026-05-04
**Purpose:** Plan the uni-assist account workflow, fee structure, and per-program coverage to minimise cost and maximise speed.

---

## What uni-assist does

uni-assist e.V. is a centralised pre-evaluation service used by ~140 German universities. For non-EU/EEA applicants with foreign Bachelor's degrees, uni-assist:

1. **Authenticates documents** (transcripts, degree, language certs).
2. **Converts grades** to the German 1.0–4.0 scale.
3. **Issues a VPD (Vorprüfungsdokumentation)** — a 1-page certificate verifying admissibility.
4. **Forwards your application** to participating universities (or you upload the VPD into a university's direct portal).

**You only pay uni-assist once for the VPD itself** (€75 for the first programme + €30 for each additional programme). The VPD is valid for one application cycle.

---

## Single-account strategy

You will create **ONE uni-assist account** at https://my.uni-assist.de/ and use it for ALL uni-assist programmes. Documents uploaded once are reused across all programmes you tick.

### Step-by-step

1. **Create profile** with passport name (exactly as in passport) + APS number.
2. **Upload documents (scan to PDF, ≤4 MB each):**
   - Bachelor's degree certificate (English translation if not already)
   - All semester transcripts
   - Class 12 certificate
   - Class 10 certificate (optional but useful)
   - APS certificate
   - IELTS TRF
   - Passport bio-data page
3. **Select target programmes** (tick boxes for each university + programme).
4. **Pay fees** via online card payment.
5. **Submit.** uni-assist takes 4–6 weeks to issue the VPD. They will email when ready.
6. **Download VPD** and use it in any university's direct portal that requires it (e.g. TU Berlin, TU Dresden, LUH).

### Per-programme add-on workflow

Once your initial VPD is issued, you can add more programmes:
- Open your uni-assist account.
- Tick the additional programme.
- Pay €30.
- uni-assist sends the (existing) VPD to the new university — no new VPD generated.

## VPD timeline (critical path)

uni-assist VPD turnaround is the constraint that drives the timeline. If you submit your VPD request **today (2026-05-04)**:
- Earliest VPD ready: 2026-06-01 (4 weeks)
- Likely VPD ready: 2026-06-15 (6 weeks)

**Implication:** The 2026-05-15 deadline programmes (OVGU × 2, TU Ilmenau) cannot wait for VPD. Workarounds:
- **OVGU Magdeburg:** uni-assist allows you to submit the application without VPD — it processes the application and the VPD in parallel. You initiate the application BEFORE the deadline (15 May) and uni-assist forwards to OVGU once VPD is done. **Confirm this in the uni-assist portal as you apply.**
- **TU Ilmenau:** No uni-assist required. Apply directly. Submit by 15 May.

For the **2026-05-31 cluster** (TU Dresden, LUH, Passau, Rostock) and **2026-06-01 cluster** (HPI direct, Bonn, Potsdam, Regensburg), the same uni-assist parallel-processing model works. Submit by the deadline; uni-assist + university will accept that VPD comes within their internal verification window (usually ~2 weeks after deadline).

**TL;DR:** Submit uni-assist application ASAP; do not wait for VPD to be issued before clicking submit.

## Common uni-assist pitfalls (Section 12 of brief + agent findings)

1. **TU Berlin requires VPD from Summer 2026 onward** — Section 12 confirmed.
2. **TU Dresden routes foreign-HZB applicants via uni-assist** despite seeming direct — Section 12 confirmed.
3. **Leibniz Hannover requires VPD for non-EU/EEA** — Section 12 confirmed.
4. **HPI Potsdam admits independently** — does NOT use uni-assist. Apply directly via hpi.de.
5. **TU Darmstadt does NOT use uni-assist** — Section 12 reinforced. Direct application via TUCaN with paper docs by post.
6. **TU Ilmenau does NOT use uni-assist** — agent finding. Direct application + €75 fee paid to TU Ilmenau.
7. **VPD must be valid for THIS application cycle.** A VPD issued for SS26 does not count for WS 26/27.
8. **Document upload size limit:** 4 MB per file. Use compression if needed.
9. **Translation requirement:** Some universities want certified translations of transcripts even if your transcripts are in English. Confirm per-university.
10. **uni-assist payment delay:** Card payments process within 24 hours; bank transfers can take 5+ days. Pay by card.
